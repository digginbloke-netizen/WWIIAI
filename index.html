WESTERN FRONT · 1944–45

Road to Berlin '44

Command the Allied campaign across a living front — then take up a rifle and fight the battles that decide it.

Strategic layer

Each turn is one week. Plan up to two offensives (three once Paris is liberated) against enemy regions bordering yours, commit troops, artillery and air support. Reinforce supplied regions with manpower, shift troops between regions and fortify the line. The German command reinforces its threatened sectors and launches its own counterattacks against weak spots. Ports and England keep your armies supplied — regions cut off from supply lose strength every week. Capture Berlin to win.

Battle layer

Any battle can be led personally in first person. Campaign troop strength becomes tickets; dozens of soldiers fight on both sides at once. Capture and hold flags A, B and C. Holding most flags drains the enemy's tickets. Your kills, captures and survival directly decide the outcome on the campaign map.

Controls: WASD move · Mouse aim · LMB fire · RMB aim down sights · Shift sprint · C crouch · Space jump · R reload · G grenade · B call artillery at crosshair · V call air strike · M big map · Tab scoreboard · Esc pause.
