WESTERN FRONT · 1944–45

Road to Berlin '44

Lead a squad of Army Rangers through a war that fights itself. Pick your targets along a living front — every gun, depot and panzer you destroy changes how the battles of the week turn out.

The front

The war runs itself, in real time: every 30 seconds the front advances a day. Allied and German commanders move armies, reinforce, fortify and attack across the map; battles grind on for days until one side breaks. Supply, weather and the winter counteroffensive all matter. The war is won when Berlin falls.

Your Rangers

Whenever you like, pick a sector on or behind the front and choose one enemy asset — artillery, flak, fuel or ammunition dumps, command posts, truck parks or panzers — as your primary objective. Then go in on foot with your squad. If a battle is raging in the sector you may stumble into it; friendly patrols roam front-line sectors. The front keeps moving while you're in the field, and every asset you destroy hits the war effort the moment it blows, objective or not: no artillery for the defenders, no fuel for counterattacks, broken command, fewer tanks. Get out through the extraction point. Rangers who fall are replaced; if you go down, you take over a squadmate.

Controls: WASD move · Mouse aim · LMB fire · RMB aim · Shift sprint · C crouch (harder to spot) · Space jump · R reload · G grenade · F plant demolition charge · Q squad hold / follow · E extract · M big map · Esc pause.
